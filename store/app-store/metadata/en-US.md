## Description

Build a steady pulse, then train each hand to play its own rhythm.

Beep Beep Beat is a landscape rhythm coach for one-hand or two-hand practice. Follow the beat, play the notes shown on screen, and build control at a comfortable tempo.

**PRACTISE YOUR WAY**

• Choose one hand or two.
• Read rhythm symbols or a score-style display.
• Explore different meters, subdivisions, tuplets, mixed rhythms, rests, and swing feel.
• Adjust beat sounds, visual flashes, haptics, and tap feedback.

**FOLLOW A GUIDED COURSE**

Work through 20 short lessons, from a steady pulse to independent hands and mixed rhythms. The first three lessons are free; a one-time purchase unlocks the full course.

**TRAIN FROM MUSIC**

Import a one- or two-part MIDI or MusicXML score, practise a built-in arrangement, and save scores for later. Score files are processed on your device.

**REVIEW AND IMPROVE**

Review accuracy by hand, replay expected notes alongside your taps, and save difficult combinations for focused practice. Challenge sessions can submit your longest clean streak to Game Center.

Settings, practice records, course progress, and imported scores are stored on your device. Game Center and purchases use Apple services.

## App Review notes (draft)

The app is designed for landscape orientation. From the home screen, tap **Start Practice** to begin, or use the activity selector to open score practice, focus practice, or the guided course. MIDI and MusicXML import accepts scores with one or two parts.

The Learn course uses the non-consumable product `com.splitbeat.learncourse.unlock`. The product must be created and made available in App Store Connect before the purchase offer appears in the app. Enable Game Center and configure leaderboard `com.splitbeat.twohandrhythm.longest_correct_streak` if reviewing the challenge leaderboard.

Review contact details and any login/demo instructions must be completed in App Store Connect.
