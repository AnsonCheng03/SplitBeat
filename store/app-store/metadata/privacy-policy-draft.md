## Information stored on your device

The app stores your settings, practice records, course progress, saved rhythm combinations, and imported scores locally on your device. If you choose to sync course progress, completed lessons and lesson ratings are saved through Apple Game Center and your iCloud account so they can be restored on your devices. Sync also uses a randomly generated installation identifier to merge progress. Apple processes this information under its terms and privacy policy. The developer does not operate the sync service or receive your progress or imported score files. If you choose to send us a support email or diagnostic export, we receive the information you include in that message.

## Optional session diagnostic export

You can choose to export a session diagnostic file and share it using the share sheet. It can contain your practice settings, app and device version details, locale, score title or lesson, generated expected notes, tap timestamps, and accuracy results. The app creates the file only when you request an export. The file is sent only to the destination you choose, and the developer does not receive a copy automatically. Review the file and recipient before sharing it.

## Apple services

If you use Game Center, Apple processes your Game Center identity and leaderboard score under Apple's terms and privacy policy. Your score and Game Center nickname may be visible to other players according to the leaderboard's settings. In-app purchases are processed by Apple; the app does not receive your payment-card details.

## Data collection and tracking

The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics, and the developer does not collect personal information through the app. Apple services may process information necessary to provide Game Center and purchases. The app does not use the microphone and does not record audio.

## Children and changes

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children. If the app's practices change, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect.
