Privacy policy

Beep Beep Beat - Rhythm Coach is published by __PUBLISHER_NAME__ (“we”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your settings, practice records, course progress, saved rhythm combinations, and imported scores locally on your device. If you choose to sync course progress, completed lessons and lesson ratings are saved through Apple Game Center and your iCloud account so they can be restored on your devices. Sync also uses a randomly generated installation identifier to merge progress. Apple processes this information under its terms and privacy policy. We do not operate the sync service or receive your progress or imported score files.

Optional session diagnostic export

You can choose to export a session diagnostic file and share it using your device’s share sheet. It may include your practice settings, app and device version details, language, score title or lesson, generated notes, tap times, and accuracy results. The file is created only when you request an export. It is sent only to the destination you choose; we do not receive a copy automatically. Review the file and recipient before sharing it.

Apple services

If you use Game Center, Apple processes your Game Center identity and leaderboard score under Apple’s terms and privacy policy. Your score and Game Center nickname may be visible to other players according to the leaderboard settings. Apple processes in-app purchases; the app does not receive your payment-card details.

Data collection and tracking

The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics, and we do not collect personal information through the app. Apple services may process information needed to provide Game Center and purchases. The app does not use the microphone or record audio.

Children and policy changes

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children. If our practices change, we will update this policy before the changes take effect.

Contact

For questions or privacy requests, email __SUPPORT_EMAIL__. If you choose to contact us, we receive the information in your message and any diagnostic file you attach. Please send only the information needed to help with your request.
