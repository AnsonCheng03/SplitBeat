# Learn course progression

The first three lessons are free. Owning the full-course purchase opens the remaining lessons and enables **Skip to this lesson** for a locked lesson.

## Course songs

The Learn screen includes a recommended original song for each lesson and a separate **Original Course Songs** library. Recommendations use the lesson's time signature. Songs reuse the score-practice player, show the full written part, and play the scored pitches along with the beat when sound is enabled. Single-track songs follow the learner's selected hand.

Song practice is supplemental: it does not award lesson stars, complete a lesson, or change course unlock progress. Song scores are authored with the score templates and do not depend on external music licensing.

## Skip eligibility

A learner can skip ahead after completing at least one lesson and meeting either mastery condition:

- The average of their best accuracy scores across lessons actually played is at least 80%.
- At least one played lesson has a five-star rating.

Skipped lessons do not count toward the accuracy average and do not earn stars. The course records them as skipped, shows that state in the course path, and lets the learner replay them later. Completing a skipped lesson replaces its skipped state with a real completion and rating.

The skip action fills only the lessons before the selected target. The target remains uncompleted and starts normally after the skip succeeds. A free learner can select a paid lesson to see the offer, but cannot use course skipping without the full-course entitlement.

## Saved progress

Skipped lesson IDs are stored separately from lesson ratings and included in the existing revision-specific Game Center/iCloud progress snapshot. Older snapshots without a skipped-lesson field remain valid and load with no skipped lessons. The content revision still determines which course progress can merge, so edits to lesson content keep their existing version boundary.

## Rhythm missions

Rhythm missions are a separate Practice Mode activity with five sequentially unlocked challenges in 4/4. Each prompt can play as sound or vibration at the current practice tempo, capped at 120 BPM, followed by either a timed tap-back attempt or a note-composer answer. The composer uses the same note symbols, rests, and score preview as Focus Practice. Tap attempts are scored against the prompt's note onsets with a tempo-scaled timing window; composed answers compare the ordered note/rest durations. Passing saves that mission ID locally and unlocks the next mission. Mission progress is independent of course stars, course purchases, and Game Center course restore.
